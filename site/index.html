What's inside

All your PokéBonk, in one place

A menu that blends right into the game. Open it with F2 any time, even mid-run.

Pokédex

All 151 Pokémon with their portraits, types and evolutions, plus what they hit hard and what hurts them, using PokéBonk's real multipliers.

Maps

Every region with its bosses, its mechanics and a ranking of all 79 partners: who to bring to Storm Coast or the Glacier, and why.

Progress

Every run is saved on its own: time, K.O.s, level, gold. Your records, your form over time and your stats per Pokémon and per map.

Discord status

Your friends see your map and your Pokémon in pixel art, with your level and K.O.s live. Nothing to set up.

Ready to play?
  1. 1Download PokeBonkApp.exe (Windows 10 or 11) or PokeBonk.apk (Android 7+). On Android, allow your browser to install unknown apps when asked.
  2. 2Run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info then Run anyway. The app isn't signed, which is normal for a fan project.
  3. 3Play. F2 opens the toolkit, and Discord updates on its own if it's running.
Download for Windows Download for Android View on GitHub Your runs stay on your device. No account, no ads. On Android the toolkit opens with its button, and Back works like Esc. The Discord status is PC only: Discord does not allow it on phones.
